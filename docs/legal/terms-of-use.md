# Enchiridion — Terms of Use (website draft)

> Draft populated from the app's actual implementation. Review with counsel
> before publishing. Last updated: September 2026.

## 1. What Enchiridion is

Enchiridion is a study companion for medical training: textbooks, clinical
calculators, instrumentation guides, OSCE stations, and exam-preparation
modules (PLAB available now; USMLE, PRIMARIES, MCCQE, AMC, NZREX, PRES,
SMLE, and MDCN are coming soon). It is an educational aid, **not a medical
device and not a substitute for professional clinical judgement**. Always
follow local protocols and senior supervision in real patient care.

## 2. Accounts

- You must provide a valid email address and verify it with the sign-up
  code to use the app.
- You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential.
- One account is bound to one primary device. You may authorise a new
  device, subject to a 30-day cooldown between switches.
- Display names must not impersonate others; duplicated display names are
  rejected at registration.

## 3. Free access

Without payment you may read limited content (including the first chapter
of each book and one calculator per category) and answer a limited number
of free practice questions per exam module (currently 10 for PLAB). Device
notes, bookmarks, and calculators within the free tier remain available
offline.

## 4. Paid access (one-time purchases, no auto-renewal)

- **Single book/module unlock:** ₦1,000 for 30 days.
- **Full subscription:** ₦1,000 for 30 days, or ₦10,000 for 365 days
  (all books and all available exam modules).
- Every purchase is a **one-time charge** completed on our website
  (enchiridion.site/app/subscribe), which opens from the app's paywall.
  Payment is processed by Flutterwave (card, bank transfer, or USSD, in
  Nigerian naira). There is no automatic renewal: when your window lapses
  you pay again to renew, and renewing before expiry extends from your
  current expiry date.
- Access is granted only after our server verifies the payment with
  Flutterwave. Tampered or replayed payment references grant nothing.
- The app checks your subscription status online; if the device cannot
  validate for more than 7 days, access automatically reverts to the free
  tier until the next successful check.

## 5. Refunds

Digital access begins immediately on payment, so purchases are
non-refundable once access is granted — except where Nigerian consumer
protection law requires otherwise, or where a payment was taken in error
(e.g. duplicate charge), in which case email support@enchiridion.site with
your payment reference.

resell, or systematically extract app content; upload unlawful or infringing
material as a profile photo; abuse the question-flagging tool; or attack,
reverse-engineer for circumvention, or disrupt the service.
